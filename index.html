Chutes & Ladders

The classic 100-square race, as a board-as-graph: a chute and a ladder are one "jump" edge, distinguished only by direction. 1-4 players, any mix of humans and bots — and the bots are not opponents with a strategy, because there is no strategy in this game: a "bot" is a die with a seating position. While the race runs, the exact probability distribution of how many more turns each player needs is computed live and shown on screen. Daily-seeded: the same day produces the same race for everyone. No economy, no spend — pure chance.
